# fred-AI-lab

Legal AI and privacy engineering projects by Fred Gomes.

Open-source experiments for legal work that a legal team can defend:
auditable criteria, measured evals and guardrails. Brazilian law first
(content in PT-BR, code in English).

## Projects

| Project | Area | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [dpa-analyzer](projects/dpa-analyzer/) | Privacy · contract review | AI agent to analyze Data Processing Agreements (DPA) |

## Build in public: "Jurídico AI-native em 60 dias"

From October to December 2026, this lab builds, in the open, the
operation of an AI-native legal department: one module every week or
two, each with a demo, a PT-BR README and an artifact a legal team can
copy. Follow along on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fredfelix/).

| Weeks | Module | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opening: the thesis and the plan | planned |
| 2–3 | Legal intake triage with an auditable rubric | planned |
| 4–5 | Personal data detector for PT-BR text ([`privacyseed-lgpd`](https://github.com/fredericofelix/privacyseed-lgpd), own repo by Privacy Seed; [demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/privacyseed/detector-lgpd)) | released (v0.1.0) |
| 6–7 | Professional-secrecy guardrail before the prompt | planned |
| 8 | NDA playbook reviewer | planned |
| 9 | Open AI maturity framework for legal teams | planned |

Synthetic or public data only. Want to apply this to your legal team?
Talk to [Lex Seed](https://www.lexseed.com.br) (Legal Ops and Legal AI)
or [Privacy Seed](https://www.privacyseed.com.br) (privacy engineering
and AI governance).
